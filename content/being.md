it's funny, it's almost easier to type these things out than to actually say them out loud. i’ve always been the person to just joke things off or switch topics when things start to feel too real. it feels like standing right on the edge of a cliff to open up, and i’m just never quite ready to actually take that step. but if you’re reading this, well, it means i finally did it. i sent this to you because i honestly didn’t know any other way to really let you in. this is me, trying to say the things i just couldn’t bring myself to say in person.

it feels strange to lay down and write this out. usually, there's some funny observation or a project update to share, often with a healthy dose of self-deprecating humor thrown in for good measure. but idk !! D:

these days, i find myself caught in a heavy stillness. a silence that isn't comforting, but suffocating. my emotions are raw and unfiltered, stripped of the laughter that once punctuated the chaos of everyday digital adventures. instead, i am left with a quiet, unyielding ache.

what used to be a source of joy, social interactions now leave me quite drained. even when i am at peace when i am with people, the effort it takes to just be with them feels like an impossible feat. each minor interaction saps whatever energy i have left. i've come to realize there's this strange pattern in me. a constant feeling of discontent.

i'm miserable, and i find myself wondering why quite often. it's like i've lost something inside of me, and it's created this emptiness that cannot be filled by anything. the constant questioning of my own worth and the inexplicable sadness that shadows my days have become much too familiar.

it's gotten to a point where humor isn't cutting it. the easy back and forths aren't happening; it's like every emotion i'm having is just so heavy. i'm typing out these words not as a call for help, but to keep track of what i'm experiencing as i'm living through this moment that's both familiar and completely foreign.
